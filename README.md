# AI4Science Seminar Series

## English

A forum for young researchers in AI for Science, initiated by Bin Cao. Real tech. Real experience. A focus on practical progress in research.

Each session features an active researcher sharing the work behind a real scientific problem: choosing methods, working through technical details, designing experiments, and validating results. We discuss lessons learned and detours along the way. Bring your research questions and leave with ideas you can try in your own work.

Our speakers and academic committee members are active researchers. The inviter also hosts each session, making room for detailed questions and discussion among peers. Researchers, students, and practitioners are welcome, as are speaker recommendations and self-nominations.

**No recordings. No replays.** Join the conversation live and take useful experience back to your research.

- Website: [https://ai4s.hk.cn](https://ai4s.hk.cn)
- WeChat official account: **SciMindBin**
- Speaker recommendations: [bincao@hkust-gz.edu.cn](mailto:bincao@hkust-gz.edu.cn)

Session topics, speakers, dates, and joining details are posted on the website as they are confirmed. Follow SciMindBin on WeChat for announcements and updates.

## 中文

由曹斌（Bin Cao）发起的年轻人的 AI for Science 论坛。真讲技术，聊透经验，重视研究工作的实际成效。

每期邀请一位一线研究者，围绕真实的科学问题，分享方法选择、技术细节、实验验证，以及研究中积累的经验和走过的弯路。我们希望参与者带着具体问题来，带着可以在自己工作中尝试的思路走。

讲者与学术委员都来自科研一线。每期由邀请人兼任主持人，为深入提问和同行讨论留出空间。欢迎研究者、学生与从业者参与，也欢迎推荐讲者或自荐。

**每期不录屏、不提供回放。** 欢迎在活动当场交流，把有用的经验带回研究。
